# VIA Tables Trainer

A single-page web app for practising multiplication tables. Open `VIA-tables-trainer.html` in any browser — no install, no internet connection needed after the page loads.

## Getting started

1. Open `VIA-tables-trainer.html` in your browser (double-click it, or drag it into a browser window).
2. On the setup screen, choose:
   - **Which tables** — e.g. 1 to 5
   - **Multiply by** — e.g. 1 to 10
   - **Time for each answer** — 3–15 seconds, or a custom number
   - **How many questions** — 10, 20, 30, 50, or endless
   - **How you answer** — say it aloud, or type it
   - **Extras** — see below
3. Tap **Start practice**.

## During a round

- A question appears with a countdown ring.
- **Say it aloud mode:** say your answer out loud, then tap **Show answer** (or press Space) once the answer appears, mark yourself **✓ right** or **✗ wrong**.
- **Type it mode:** type your answer and tap **Check answer** (or press Enter) — it's graded automatically.
- Use **Pause** to stop the clock at any time.

### Keyboard shortcuts
| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Space | Show the answer |
| → or Y | Mark right |
| ← or N | Mark wrong |
| Enter | Check / next question |
| P | Pause / resume |

## Extra features

- **Bring missed questions back** — a wrong answer reappears a few questions later in the same round.
- **Weak spots list** — questions you get wrong are saved on this device (in your browser's local storage) so you can practise them again later. They clear once you answer correctly.
- **Ask both ways** — mixes, e.g., 7 × 4 with 4 × 7.
- **Read questions aloud** — uses your device's built-in text-to-speech.
- **Sound effects** — countdown ticks and right/wrong tones.
- **Recent rounds** — your last few scores are shown on the setup screen.
- **Light / dark theme** — toggle with the moon/sun icon, top right.

## Data & privacy

All progress (weak spots and recent scores) is stored only in your browser's local storage on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and nothing is shared between devices or browsers.
